# Day 1

## St Francis Church

![](./image4.jpg)

The first thing to do today is of course to go to church! The mass today is somewhat fast, because it does not have a choir.

In the homily today, the priest speaks about worries in life, and lists the example of the recent election. Interesting that he mentions it, what happened to it recently?

The morning here is freezing cold, it was as if snow was about to fall. Nevertheless, the trams here are very romantic 😊

![](./image6.jpg)

## State library Victoria

![](./image10.jpg)

This library is really huge, with lots of areas and a great variety of books.

![](./image11.jpg)

There was a demonstration against the unfortunate murder of a Chinese delivery rider, due to a conflict that arose from unprovoked damage to his bicycle. That happened 2 days before, Nov 6. The demonstration demands for greater justice and security in Melbourne.

Two chill men are playing chess on a very huge board. It is unfortunately not in my repertoire, so I dare not challenge.

![](./image12.jpg)

The library structure is interesting. I found a history book on WWII here, and spent some time reading 2 chapters.

![](./image13.jpg)

There are a lot of areas for books, books and books. And study areas too. I would recommend spending a lot of time here, if you love to pick a nice book to read.

## Penguins

We sat at the theatre for a while before the penguins came. They didn’t allow photos and the penguins were so far away. Also, they look very similar to the seagulls, so it was difficult to tell if they had landed. So I thought this ticket was wasted.

![](./image32.jpg)

Turns out, the real theatre was at the boardwalk back towards the visitor centre. These cute little penguins are waddling from the seashore back to their burrows \>\_\< Finally, that was worth the ticket.

## Cycling

This is the highlight of the day! It was exhilarating, hot, and memorable!

![](./image40.jpg)

Some footage of me riding on my bike! The bike is very good, it can speed up to the speed of cars. However, it requires lots of energy to maintain that high gear, so I maintain a lower speed.

![](./image41.jpg)

Some scenes on the way!

This time, I am more prepared for cycling than the last time in Japan. However, I put in too much strength to race with the cars at the start, so I had less energy to ride fast on the way back. My leg is almost cramped oops.

The weather is super hot: it was very sunny, and the temperature was close to 30 degrees. Oh wow, it was still so cold in the morning so I put on 2 layers of undetachable top, and now I’m stuck in sweat.

Anyway, I managed to go way beyond the initial target of 4-hour cycling: Brighton beach boxes. I managed to reach near Ricketts Marine Sanctuary, and with the multiple lengthy stops on the way, I still managed to cycle around 40km.
